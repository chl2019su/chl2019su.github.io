I am a robotics engineer who wants to design a future where people and robots coexist naturally. My work spans the full hardware-development cycle, from mechanical architecture and CAD to analysis, prototyping, assembly, testing, and production-oriented refinement.

My experience includes humanoid robots, human-robot interaction platforms, multi-joint rehabilitation systems, and biomimetic flying robots. Across these projects, I focus on practical mechanisms that balance performance, reliability, manufacturability, and the way people actually interact with a product.

## Focus areas

- Humanoid robot mechanisms and integrated hardware
- Rehabilitation robot redesign and production transfer
- Human-robot interaction products
- CAD, mechanism design, and structural analysis
- Prototype validation and design-for-manufacturing
