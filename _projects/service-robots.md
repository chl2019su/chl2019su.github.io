This collection covers several robots designed around direct interaction with people.

## Selected systems

- **ABLE and ERICA** — wheeled guide robots with interactive displays, articulated arms, and user-facing industrial design.
- **DIANA** — a directional guide robot whose waist structure was revised for 360° motion; the project received awards in both original and autonomous-driving categories at a 2018 ski-robot competition.
- **Pinocchio** — a conversational robot that paired a familiar character form with speech-recognition and dialogue behavior.

Across these platforms, my work emphasized mechanical packaging, mechanism design, collaboration with industrial designers, and the practical demands of human-facing robots.
