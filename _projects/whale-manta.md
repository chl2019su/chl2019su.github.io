Whale and Manta are helium-filled biomimetic robots that translate the motion of marine animals into lightweight indoor flight mechanisms.

## Contribution

- Developed mechanisms that reproduce swimming-like motion with flexible structures.
- Designed internal frames with weight and buoyancy constraints in mind.
- Produced exterior shapes and structural parts for inflatable bodies.
- Collaborated with controls engineers to achieve stable, controllable flight.

The robots were developed as large-scale exhibition platforms, combining biological inspiration with practical lightweight engineering.
