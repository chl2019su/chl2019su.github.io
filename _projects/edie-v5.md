EDIE v5 is a compact, character-driven interaction robot that combines vision-based user tracking, expressive displays, touch sensing, and mobile motion.

## Contribution

- Designed the internal structure and external cover around cameras, displays, force sensors, speakers, LEDs, and cooling.
- Packaged electronics for reliable assembly and service access.
- Added a detachable hot-swap battery architecture.
- Coordinated a soft fabric exterior with the industrial-design team.
- Refined the enclosure for durability, tooling, and production.

The result balances a friendly visual identity with the mechanical and electrical constraints of an interactive product.
