ALICE SE is a compact humanoid platform developed to evaluate how an articulated toe affects bipedal walking efficiency.

## Contribution

- Designed a four-bar toe mechanism that distributes loads across four links.
- Used springs for energy storage and damping during the gait cycle.
- Designed interchangeable toe and sole modules for matched experiments.
- Supported mechanical validation of the walking platform and joint design.

## System and result

- Height: 52.6 cm
- Weight: 3.66 kg
- Degrees of freedom: 14
- The articulated-toe configuration reduced measured electrical energy use by 13.23% in the reported comparison.
- Joint research with the UCLA Robotics & Mechanisms Laboratory (RoMeLa).
