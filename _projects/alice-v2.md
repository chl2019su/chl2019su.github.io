ALICE v2 is an autonomous humanoid soccer robot designed for natural walking, robust actuation, and integrated sensing.

## Contribution

- Designed the 6-DoF leg architecture and joint arrangement.
- Developed lightweight structural parts using AL6061 and selected bearings and reducers around the required loads.
- Integrated cameras, force-torque sensors, batteries, and the main control computer within a compact body.
- Supported fabrication, assembly, motion testing, and RoboCup preparation.

## System

- Height: 136 cm
- Weight: 20 kg
- Degrees of freedom: 20
- Tools: Creo / Pro/ENGINEER and ROS
