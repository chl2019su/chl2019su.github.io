K30A and U30A are rehabilitation robots for knee, shoulder, wrist, and hand exercise. The project focused on redesigning existing systems for improved performance, serviceability, safety, and production.

## Contribution

- Improved the knee robot gearbox, reducing backlash and increasing speed and torque capability.
- Redesigned load-cell mounting, covers, the base frame, and adjustable supports.
- Resolved actuator interference and patient leg-alignment issues through a size-adjustable structure.
- Packaged electronics and sensors around clinical access and maintainability constraints.
- Supported prototype builds, hardware and software verification, production transfer, and certification documentation.

The work culminated in a completed K30A development cycle and production transfer, including medical-device manufacturing and quality-system activities described in the source portfolio.
