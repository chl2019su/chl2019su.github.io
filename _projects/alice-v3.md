ALICE v3 extends the earlier platform with additional waist mobility, revised lower-body geometry, and a more capable upper body.

## Contribution

- Added waist yaw and pitch joints to expand body motion.
- Redesigned the hip, knee, and foot structures while preserving compatibility with established joint modules.
- Increased knee range of motion from 90° to 120° and optimized motor placement around the battery.
- Designed articulated arms for a more complete humanoid configuration.
- Participated in assembly and full-body motion testing.

## System

- Height: 137 cm
- Weight: 24 kg
- Degrees of freedom: 22
- Result: 3rd place, RoboCup 2021 Humanoid AdultSize
